# Data Deletion Instructions — Sincerely Yours

The "Sincerely Yours" app does not collect or store personal data from Facebook users.
It only publishes posts to the "Sincerely Yours" Facebook Page and reads aggregate statistics for those posts.

If you have interacted with the app or the Page and want any related data removed:

1. Go to your Facebook **Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites**.
2. Find **Sincerely Yours** and click **Remove**.
3. Optionally, send a message to the "Sincerely Yours" Facebook Page asking for deletion. Any record linked to you will be deleted within 30 days and you will receive a confirmation.
