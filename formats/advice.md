# Format Brief: Advice from 1905

- **Target Share:** 15% (starting)
- **Word Count:** 100–180 words
- **Visual:** Newspaper-page clipping or card showing the original "Answers to Correspondents" or agony column
- **Structure:**
  1. Hook (The reader's predicament in 1 line, e.g. "A young clerk writes to ask if three letters a week is too forward...")
  2. The historical dilemma (condensed summary of the 1900s letter)
  3. The Edwardian/Victorian columnist's verdict (strict etiquette, brutal honesty, or surprising wisdom)
  4. "What we'd say now" (a witty, warm modern perspective in the persona voice)
  5. Source line (e.g., "From the advice column in The Evening Star, 1905.")
- **Rule:** High comment driver—ask the reader subtly how they would handle the situation.
