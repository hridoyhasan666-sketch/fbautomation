# Format: The Forgotten Habit

- **Length:** 80–160 words
- **Goal:** Nostalgia + usefulness. A habit our grandparents and great-grandparents had that most of us lost, and why it is worth bringing back.
- **Structure:**
  1. Hook line naming the habit in a curious way (under 15 words).
  2. What the habit looked like in daily life (concrete, sensory, 2–3 sentences).
  3. Why it quietly worked — the human reason, not a lecture.
  4. One small, practical way to try it this week.
  5. Close with a warm question ("Did your grandparents do this?" style, but phrased freshly).
