# Format Brief: Guess the Year

- **Target Share:** 10% (starting)
- **Word Count:** 40–80 words
- **Visual:** Clipping with the date banner, year, and obvious timestamps digitally masked out
- **Structure:**
  1. Hook: "Can you guess which year this was printed?"
  2. 2–3 bullet clues or intriguing details from the text (prices, slang, technology, social dispute)
  3. Call-to-action: "Drop your best guess below—answer revealed tomorrow!"
  4. Automatic 24h follow-up post or comment reply scheduled with the answer.
- **Rule:** High algorithm engagement booster through comment volume.
