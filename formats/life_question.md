# Format: The Question

- **Length:** 40–90 words
- **Goal:** Comments and shares. A short, thoughtful "would you / which is better" post about life then vs now.
- **Structure:**
  1. Hook: a clear trade-off between old life and modern life (under 15 words).
  2. 2–4 short lines laying out both sides fairly, with concrete details.
  3. End with the question itself, easy to answer in one word or one sentence.
- **Rule:** Must be genuinely debatable. No obvious right answer, no guilt-tripping.
