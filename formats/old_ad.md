# Format Brief: Old Ad of the Day

- **Target Share:** 15% (starting)
- **Word Count:** 40–90 words
- **Visual:** Clean crop of a striking public-domain historical advertisement (bizarre patent medicine, curious invention, lost trade, or luxury good)
- **Structure:**
  1. Hook (highlighting the strangest claim or promise in the ad)
  2. 1–2 sentences placing the product in its historical context
  3. The Modern Mirror (e.g. how modern wellness influencers or tech startups sell the exact same promise)
  4. Source line (e.g., "From an advertisement in The San Francisco Call, 1899.")
- **Rule:** High engagement / share potential. Let the image do the heavy lifting.
