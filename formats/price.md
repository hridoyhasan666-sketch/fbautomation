# Format Brief: Then vs. Now Price

- **Target Share:** 15% (starting)
- **Word Count:** 50–120 words
- **Visual:** Real vintage advertisement/price crop with clean modern price overlay: "1910: 12¢ → Today: $4.10"
- **Structure:**
  1. Hook (stating the item and what it cost back then)
  2. Context (1-2 sentences on wages, daily labor, or what that money represented)
  3. The Modern Inflation Math (calculated strictly via CPI data, never invented by AI)
  4. One thought-provoking closing line on purchasing power vs modern life
  5. Source line (e.g., "From a grocery ad in The Washington Times, 1912.")
- **Rule:** The AI must NEVER calculate the inflation conversion itself; it receives the pre-calculated figures from the CPI engine.
