# Format: A Short Story (Fiction)

- **Length:** 150–280 words
- **Goal:** Emotion and shares. A tiny, complete story of an ordinary person ~100 years ago whose feeling a modern reader instantly recognises.
- **Structure:**
  1. Hook: a single vivid moment (under 15 words).
  2. A scene with sensory detail and one clear want (a letter, a debt, a goodbye, a small hope).
  3. A turn or quiet revelation.
  4. A final line that connects to the reader's own life without explaining the moral.
- **Mandatory:** The body's last line must be exactly: "A short story inspired by everyday life a century ago."
- **Rule:** Invented characters only. No real people, no real named events presented as fact.
