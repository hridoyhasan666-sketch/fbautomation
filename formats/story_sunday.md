# Format Brief: Story Sunday (Fiction)

- **Target Share:** 15% (Sunday only)
- **Word Count:** 200–350 words
- **Visual:** Vintage-style period print illustration or atmospheric darkened clipping
- **Structure:**
  1. Compelling narrative hook
  2. Full immersive scene exploring the emotional heart of an old record
  3. Sensory depth: sounds, weather, unspoken longing, dialogue subtext
  4. Bittersweet or lingering ending
  5. Mandatory Transparency Line: "Inspired by a real item in [Paper/Region, Year]. Characters and fictionalized scenes are created for Story Sunday."
- **Rule:** Clearly labeled as fiction to prevent misinformation flags while showcasing literary craft.
