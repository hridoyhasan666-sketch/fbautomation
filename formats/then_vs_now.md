# Format: Then vs. Now

- **Length:** 90–170 words
- **Goal:** Make the reader see their own everyday life differently by putting it next to how people did the same thing ~100 years ago.
- **Structure:**
  1. Hook line: one concrete, surprising contrast (under 15 words).
  2. THEN: 2–3 short sentences painting how it worked back then, with tangible everyday detail.
  3. NOW: 1–2 sentences on how we do it today.
  4. The turn: one short standalone line with the insight — what we gained, what we lost, or what never changed.
  5. Close with ONE simple question readers can answer in a few words in the comments.
