# Format Brief: True Retelling

- **Target Share:** 30% (starting)
- **Word Count:** 120–250 words
- **Visual:** Newspaper-page card with headline/text overlay
- **Structure:**
  1. Hook (1 sentence, under 15 words, anomaly or striking moment)
  2. Narrative body (2-3 short paragraphs retelling the true event vividly)
  3. The Modern Bridge (2-line modern turn connecting to modern human life)
  4. Source line (e.g., "From a real report in the Omaha Daily Bee, 1904.")
- **Strict Citation Rules:**
  - Every factual claim (names/places/amounts/events) must cite an exact or near-exact OCR span in the clipping.
  - Zero invented historical facts.
  - People must be renamed to protect privacy.
- **Tone:** Empathetic Anthropologist or Razor-Sharp Observer.
